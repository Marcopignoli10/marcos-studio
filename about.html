About

I have always been a creative person, whether its drawing, painting, sculpting, designing or any form of art was always resonant to my soul. It all started when I was a kid, I always doodled during classes and loved art classes but I wasn't into art as much as I am now. During covid, boredom made me explore art styles I didn't even know I could draw with my very own hands. I started with references from comic panels and from there, every single day became a day I drew a new sketch. Truth be told, after my sketches started getting attention from girls I started venturing more into art and found styles that girls appreciated more. From there I made more comic panel art to fashion art, cartoonish art and now digital art pieces.
